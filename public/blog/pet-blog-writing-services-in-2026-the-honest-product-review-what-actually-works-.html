What “Content Gap Analysis” Should Actually Mean for Pet Brands

When someone says “content gap,” we translate it into business terms. It means you found the topics your competitors already cover, and buyers already read, and you finally decide to publish the missing pieces.

For pet blog writing services, a real Content Gap Analysis should cover:

  • Category-level intent gaps (what pet parents search before they buy)
  • Product-usage gaps (how customers describe symptoms, goals, and results)
  • Comparison and decision gaps (what they ask when they are choosing between options)
  • On-site linking paths so your articles connect to collections and products in a way that feels natural

If the provider only produces “general pet articles,” you will spend money building awareness instead of demand. And in 2026, demand is the whole point.
